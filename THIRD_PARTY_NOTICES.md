# Third-party notices and asset scope

Dependencies are installed from the package registry, not vendored into this repository. They retain their package licenses and notices. The lockfile records exact versions; local verification generates a private dependency-license inventory for owner review. Important direct dependencies include Astro (MIT), TypeScript (Apache-2.0), React (MIT), Tailwind CSS (MIT), Playwright (Apache-2.0), Lighthouse (Apache-2.0), and ESLint (MIT).

Sharp uses Apache-2.0 and its installed native libvips distribution includes LGPL-3.0-or-later components. Three transitive package entries declare MPL-2.0. These packages and native binaries are not vendored here; retain their upstream notices and review their terms if distributing an installed/bundled application. The repository's MIT license does not replace dependency licenses.

The sample SVG illustrations and synthetic social cards are project-created demonstration assets. Screenshots show only these synthetic local pages. System font stacks avoid distributing proprietary font files. No stock photos, customer logos, founder portrait, real recordings, paid templates or third-party business data are included.

The Places adapter references Google's official API; this license grants no rights to provider services, trademarks or API-returned content. Provider terms and billing rules apply to any optional live use.

The upstream private source had no checked-in license. The owner selected the included MIT license for this first-party extraction on October 12, 2026. Contributor/ownership review remains part of publication review; this is not an assertion that the private upstream was already MIT-licensed.
